    • About Me

    I am a Senior Machine Learning Scientist at Apple, where I work on streaming ASR and full-duplex speech LLMs. Before that, I spent four years at Meta (FAIR and Reality Labs), working on large-scale multilingual speech translation — including UnitY, SeamlessM4T, and Seamless — and on real-time multimodal human-AI interaction. I received my Ph.D. from the Graduate School of Informatics, Kyoto University in 2021, supervised by Prof. Tatsuya Kawahara.
    My CV is available here.
